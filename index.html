What it is
Grass-fed, locally sourced whey protein with traceable ingredients. Simple. Transparent. No proprietary blends, no fillers, no mystery. Just reliable protein that works as hard as you do — and costs what it should.
02
What it isn't
Not a rage brand. Not a clinical white-label tub with a serif font and nothing to say. Not a 47-ingredient label designed to impress rather than perform. Not a personality. Just good protein, honestly made.
03

You train 3–5 times a week. Gym, running, sport, whatever. You take it seriously without being weird about it. You want to feel good and stay capable — strong for life, not just for right now. That's the whole thing, really.
